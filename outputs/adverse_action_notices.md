# Sample adverse action notices

These notices follow the structure of an ECOA Regulation B adverse action notice. The principal reasons are listed in order of importance as the model saw them.

## Applicant 4

Your application for credit has been declined. The principal reasons for this decision are:

1. Delinquent past or present credit obligations with others (code R04)
2. Excessive obligations in relation to income (code R02)
3. Income insufficient for amount of credit requested (code R01)
4. Proportion of balances to credit limits on revolving accounts is too high (code R03)

Model score 0.29 against a threshold of 0.5. Key inputs: income 50722, debt to income 0.48, utilisation 0.39, months since delinquency 18, inquiries 1.

You have the right to a statement of specific reasons and to request a copy of the information used in this decision.
